Form validation & local image preview

A small demo of two common front-end techniques: checking form fields before letting a user continue, and letting a user preview chosen photos entirely on their own device.

1. Form fields

2. Choose photos to preview

The browser's own file picker is doing the work here — a page can never read your photo library on its own. The user has to open the picker and pick files explicitly, every time. This demo then uses URL.createObjectURL() to show the chosen images directly in the page, in memory, with no upload involved.
